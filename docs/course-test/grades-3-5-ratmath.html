  19. 19.Area and perimeterAgrees
    A rectangular garden is 7127\tfrac12721​ feet by 4 feet. Find its area and its perimeter.
    Independent answer
    Area 30 ft230\ \text{ft}^230 ft2; perimeter 2(712+4)=232(7\tfrac12 + 4) = 232(721​+4)=23 ft.
    RatMath code
    const L = units.Q("7..1/2 ft"), W = units.Q("4 ft");
[L.multiply(W), L.add(W).multiply(R`2`)]
    Output
    [30 ft^2, 23 ft]
