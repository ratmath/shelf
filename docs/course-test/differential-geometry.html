The helix (speed, curvature, torsion, length, Frenet frame), curvature and the osculating circle of a parabola, its evolute, total curvature of a circle, the torus (first fundamental form, Gaussian curvature on the outer and inner rims, area), the sphere and the hyperbolic half-plane, a cylinder, a saddle and the catenoid as a minimal surface, Christoffel symbols in polar coordinates, a hyperbolic geodesic, holonomy of parallel transport round a latitude, and Gauss–Bonnet.

Twenty exercises of the kind this course sets. Answers are on the independent answers page; the same exercises done with RatMath are on the RatMath answers page.
