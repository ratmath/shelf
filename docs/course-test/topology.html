Topology: test

TestIndependent answersRatMath answers

Counting the topologies on a three-point set, the taxicab unit ball, connectedness, compactness and the fundamental group (which are proofs), winding numbers and the degree of (z^3), the one-dimensional Brouwer fixed point theorem, Euler characteristics of polyhedra and graphs, the first homology of the torus, the projective plane and the Klein bottle from their cell structures, the genus of a surface from its Euler characteristic, and the Cantor set and Sierpinski carpet.

Twenty exercises of the kind this course sets. Answers are on the independent answers page; the same exercises done with RatMath are on the RatMath answers page.

  1. 1.Finite topologies
    How many topologies are there on a three-point set?
  2. 2.Metrics
    Describe the unit ball of the taxicab metric d(p,q)=∣p1−q1∣+∣p2−q2∣d(p, q) = |p_1 - q_1| + |p_2 - q_2|d(p,q)=∣p1​−q1​∣+∣p2​−q2​∣ in the plane. What is its "π\piπ" (half the perimeter of the unit circle, measured in the same metric)?
  3. 3.A bounded metric
    Show d′(x,y)=∣x−y∣1+∣x−y∣d'(x, y) = \dfrac{|x - y|}{1 + |x - y|}d′(x,y)=1+∣x−y∣∣x−y∣​ is a metric on R\mathbb RR giving the same topology.
  4. 4.Connectedness and compactness
    Is [0,1][0, 1][0,1] compact? Is (0,1)(0, 1)(0,1)? Is Q\mathbb QQ connected?
  5. 5.Winding numbers
    Find the winding number about 0 of the closed polygon 1→i→−1→−i→11 \to i \to -1 \to -i \to 11→i→−1→−i→1, of the same path traversed twice, and of the triangle 3→3+i→2+i→33 \to 3 + i \to 2 + i \to 33→3+i→2+i→3.
  6. 6.Degree of a map
    What is the degree of z↦z3z \mapsto z^3z↦z3 on the unit circle (how many times does the image wind round 0)?
  7. 7.Fixed point theorem
    Show every continuous f:[0,1]→[0,1]f: [0, 1] \to [0, 1]f:[0,1]→[0,1] has a fixed point, and find the fixed point of cos⁡\coscos on [0,1][0, 1][0,1].
  8. 8.Euler characteristic of polyhedra
    Compute V−E+FV - E + FV−E+F for the tetrahedron, cube and icosahedron.
  9. 9.Euler characteristic of a graph
    For the Petersen graph as a 1-dimensional complex, find χ\chiχ and the rank of H1H_1H1​.
  10. 10.Homology of the torus
    The torus has a cell structure with one 0-cell, two 1-cells a,ba, ba,b and one 2-cell attached along aba−1b−1aba^{-1}b^{-1}aba−1b−1. Find H1H_1H1​.
  11. 11.Homology of the projective plane
    RP2\mathbb{RP}^2RP2 has one cell in each dimension 0, 1, 2, the 2-cell attached along aaaaaa. Find H1H_1H1​.
  12. 12.Homology of the Klein bottle
    The Klein bottle is one 0-cell, 1-cells a,ba, ba,b, and a 2-cell attached along aba−1baba^{-1}baba−1b. Find H1H_1H1​.
  13. 13.Smith normal form and torsion
    A cell complex has ∂2=(2468)\partial_2 = \begin{pmatrix}2&4\\6&8\end{pmatrix}∂2​=(26​48​) (in bases of 2-cells and 1-cells) and ∂1=0\partial_1 = 0∂1​=0. Find H1H_1H1​.
  14. 14.Classification of surfaces
    A closed orientable surface has a triangulation with 8 vertices, 24 edges and 14 triangles. Which surface is it?
  15. 15.Genus of a graph
    What is the genus of K5K_5K5​ (the least genus of a surface it embeds in)?
  16. 16.The Cantor set
    What is the total length of the nnnth stage of the Cantor construction? What is the measure of the Cantor set?
  17. 17.Fractal dimension
    Find the similarity dimension of the Sierpinski carpet.
  18. 18.Fundamental groups
    What are the fundamental groups of the circle, the torus and RP2\mathbb{RP}^2RP2?
  19. 19.Stereographic projection
    Under stereographic projection from the north pole of the unit sphere, where does the point (35,0,45)(\tfrac35, 0, \tfrac45)(53​,0,54​) go?
  20. 20.A triangulated torus
    Möbius and Császár's minimal triangulation of the torus has 7 vertices, 21 edges and 14 triangles. Check its Euler characteristic.
