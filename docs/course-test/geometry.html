Geometry (high school): test

TestIndependent answersRatMath answers

Midpoints, distance and slopes in the plane, classifying triangles and polygons, similarity, special right triangles, right-triangle trigonometry and the laws of sines and cosines, circles (equations, arcs, sectors, inscribed angles, tangents), transformations, volume and surface area, triangle centres, and proof.

Twenty exercises of the kind this course sets. Answers are on the independent answers page; the same exercises done with RatMath are on the RatMath answers page.
