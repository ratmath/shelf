    Find gcd⁡(252,198)\gcd(252, 198)gcd(252,198) and integers x,yx, yx,y with 252x+198y=gcd⁡(252,198)252x + 198y = \gcd(252, 198)252x+198y=gcd(252,198).
    Answer
    252=1⋅198+54252 = 1\cdot198 + 54252=1⋅198+54, 198=3⋅54+36198 = 3\cdot54 + 36198=3⋅54+36, 54=1⋅36+1854 = 1\cdot36 + 1854=1⋅36+18, 36=2⋅1836 = 2\cdot1836=2⋅18. So the gcd is 18, and back-substituting gives 18=4⋅252−5⋅19818 = 4\cdot252 - 5\cdot19818=4⋅252−5⋅198.

  17. 17.Frobenius number
    Chicken nuggets come in boxes of 6, 9 and 20. What is the largest number that cannot be bought exactly?
    Answer
