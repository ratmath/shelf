Course Test · School: high school

High-school statistics (AP Statistics): test

TestIndependent answersRatMath answers

Summary statistics, z-scores and normal calculations, correlation and least-squares residuals, binomial probabilities, expected value, conditional probability and Bayes' rule, sampling distributions, confidence intervals for a proportion and a mean, and tests for a proportion, a mean, goodness of fit and independence.

Twenty exercises of the kind this course sets. Answers are on the independent answers page; the same exercises done with RatMath are on the RatMath answers page.

  1. 1.Mean and standard deviation
    For the data 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9 find the mean, the sample standard deviation and the population standard deviation.
  2. 2.z-scores
    A test has mean 72 and standard deviation 8. What is the z-score of a mark of 86?
  3. 3.Normal probability
    Find P(Z<1.75)P(Z < 1.75)P(Z<1.75) for a standard normal ZZZ, to four decimals.
  4. 4.Normal proportion between values
    Heights are normal with mean 64 in and standard deviation 2.5 in. What proportion lie between 60 and 68 inches?
  5. 5.Inverse normal
    Scores are normal with mean 500 and standard deviation 100. What score is the 90th percentile?
  6. 6.Correlation
    Find the correlation of (1,2),(2,4),(3,5),(4,4),(5,6)(1, 2), (2, 4), (3, 5), (4, 4), (5, 6)(1,2),(2,4),(3,5),(4,4),(5,6).
  7. 7.Residuals
    For the same data the least-squares line is y^=0.8x+1.8\hat y = 0.8x + 1.8y^​=0.8x+1.8. Find the residual at (3,5)(3, 5)(3,5).
  8. 8.Binomial probability
    XXX is binomial with n=10n = 10n=10, p=0.3p = 0.3p=0.3. Find P(X=3)P(X = 3)P(X=3).
  9. 9.Binomial cumulative
    For the same XXX, find P(X≤2)P(X \le 2)P(X≤2).
  10. 10.Expected value
    A game costs \$1. Roll a die: on a 6 you get \$6 back (a \$5 gain), otherwise nothing. What is the expected gain?
  11. 11.Conditional probability
    Of 50 students who studied, 45 passed; of 50 who did not, 15 passed. Find P(pass∣studied)P(\text{pass}\mid\text{studied})P(pass∣studied) and P(studied∣pass)P(\text{studied}\mid\text{pass})P(studied∣pass).
  12. 12.Bayes' rule
    A disease affects 1% of people. A test detects it 95% of the time and gives a false positive 5% of the time. Given a positive test, what is the probability of disease?
  13. 13.Sampling distribution of the mean
    A population has mean 50 and standard deviation 12. For samples of 36, find P(xˉ>53)P(\bar x > 53)P(xˉ>53).
  14. 14.Confidence interval for a proportion
    In a poll, 540 of 900 people say yes. Give a 95% confidence interval for the proportion.
  15. 15.Confidence interval for a mean
    A sample of 16 has mean 25.3 and standard deviation 4.2. Give a 95% ttt interval for the mean.
  16. 16.Test for a proportion
    Test H0:p=0.5H_0: p = 0.5H0​:p=0.5 against p≠0.5p \ne 0.5p=0.5 when 62 of 100 trials succeed. Give zzz and the P-value.
  17. 17.One-sample t test
    Test H0:μ=5H_0: \mu = 5H0​:μ=5 against μ≠5\mu \ne 5μ=5 for the data 5.1, 4.9, 5.6, 5.8, 6.0, 5.3, 5.5, 5.9.
  18. 18.Goodness of fit
    A die rolled 60 times gives counts 8, 12, 9, 11, 6, 14. Is it fair? Give χ2\chi^2χ2 and the P-value.
  19. 19.Test of independence
    Test whether studying and passing are independent, using the table of exercise 11 (no continuity correction).
  20. 20.At least one
    What is the probability of at least one 6 in four rolls of a die?
