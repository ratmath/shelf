Abstract algebra II: rings, fields and Galois theory: test

TestIndependent answersRatMath answers

Polynomial gcds and factoring over (mathbb Q), Eisenstein's criterion, quotient rings that are or are not fields, Gröbner bases, unique factorisation failing in (mathbb Z[sqrt{-5}]), arithmetic in (mathrm{GF}(8)), Conway polynomials, minimal polynomials and primitive elements, degrees of towers, Galois groups of cubics, quartics and quintics, the discriminant test, solving by radicals, ruler-and-compass constructions, cyclotomic polynomials and the Smith normal form.

Twenty exercises of the kind this course sets. Answers are on the independent answers page; the same exercises done with RatMath are on the RatMath answers page.
