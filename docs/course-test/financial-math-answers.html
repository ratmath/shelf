  1. 1.Effective rate
    What effective annual rate corresponds to 6% compounded monthly?
    Answer
    (1+0.06/12)12−1=1.00512−1≈6.16778%(1 + 0.06/12)^{12} - 1 = 1.005^{12} - 1 \approx 6.16778\%(1+0.06/12)12−1=1.00512−1≈6.16778%.
  2. 2.Compound interest
    What does \$1000 grow to in 10 years at 5% compounded annually?
    Answer
    1000⋅1.0510≈$1628.891000 \cdot 1.05^{10} \approx \$1628.891000⋅1.0510≈$1628.89.

  18. 18.Option strategies
    A straddle buys a call (premium 5) and a put (premium 4), both struck at 100. Where does it break even?
