Disagreements (6)

DisagreesGrades K–2: number sense and early arithmetic 8
Rounding
Core rounds half to even unless told otherwise, so 65 goes to 60; school rounding is half up, roundTo(-1, "half-up"), which gives 70. Not a bug, but the default surprises anyone checking school work. (Also: Rational#round() and floor() return a bigint, not an Integer, so .multiply cannot follow them.)
DisagreesGrade 6: ratios, negative numbers and expressions 19
Range and interquartile range
stats computes quartiles by interpolation (the default linear method, as in R and NumPy): Q1=7.75Q_1 = 7.75Q1​=7.75, Q3=15Q_3 = 15Q3​=15, IQR 7.25. School textbooks and the TI-84 take the medians of the lower and upper halves, and stats has no method for that. (method: "hazen" happens to agree here, with eight values, but not in general.)
DisagreesCalculus II 7

DisagreesCalculus II 8
Improper integral, unbounded integrand
symbolic finds 2x2\sqrt x2x​ but declines the definite integral (value null: the integrand cannot be bounded on the interval). That is honest, but the improper integral converges and the antiderivative has a limit at 0.

Partial answers (33)

PartlyGrades K–2: number sense and early arithmetic 6
Place value
Core has no digit or place-value helper; the code takes the tens digit with integer division and remainder, which is the arithmetic behind place value rather than the idea itself.
PartlyGrades K–2: number sense and early arithmetic 11
Money
The units catalogue has no coins (quarter, dime, penny), so the coin values are typed in by hand.
PartlyGrades K–2: number sense and early arithmetic 12
Time
Units convert 195 minutes to 13/4 hours exactly, but there is no clock time: reading 13/4 h as 3:15 is done by hand.
PartlyGrade 7: proportional reasoning and rational numbers 16
Surface area
geo's solids (Polyhedron) are the Platonic solids and their kin; there is no box with given edges, so the formula is typed in.
PartlyGrade 8: linear equations, functions and the Pythagorean theorem 9
Equation of a line
geo writes a line as ax+by+c=0ax + by + c = 0ax+by+c=0; the slope–intercept form is read off by hand.
PartlyAlgebra I 3
Compound inequality
solveInequality does not read a chained inequality (-3 < 2x + 1 <= 7 is refused as "not an inequality"), and RealSet has no intersection, so the two halves are solved separately and intersected by hand.
PartlyAlgebra I 15
Completing the square
The vertex comes out (from where the slope is zero), but there is no 'complete the square' or vertex-form rewrite: the form (x−4)2−5(x - 4)^2 - 5(x−4)2−5 is not produced.
PartlyGeometry (high school) 4
Polygon angles
The formula is typed in. geo's regularPolygon has exact vertices, but no angle-sum or interior-angle property was found.
PartlyGeometry (high school) 11
Centre and radius
graphics recognises the curve as a circle, but nothing returns its centre and radius from the general equation (geo builds circles from a centre and a point; geo.Conic classifies projective conics). Completing the square is done by hand.
PartlyGeometry (high school) 13
Inscribed angles
The inscribed angle theorem is in geo (geo.proofs.inscribedAngle() proves it), but using it here is just halving.
PartlyGeometry (high school) 14
Tangent length
geo gives the tangent lines; their length to the point of tangency is read off by hand (12).
PartlyAlgebra II 17
Composition
Composition is done by pasting text into text; symbolic has substitution (subs) for numbers but no function composition as such.
PartlyPrecalculus and trigonometry 3
Trigonometric equation
symbolic merges the three families into one, x=2nπ3x = \tfrac{2n\pi}{3}x=32nπ​, which is the same set; picking the members in [0,2π)[0, 2\pi)[0,2π) (n=0,1,2n = 0, 1, 2n=0,1,2) is left to the reader.
PartlyPrecalculus and trigonometry 6
Sinusoids
No function reads amplitude, period and phase from a sinusoid. analytic's certified extrema show the maxima 4 and minima −2 (so amplitude 3 and midline 1) and their spacing (period π); the phase shift is read by hand.

Inverse trigonometry
symbolic leaves arcsin⁡(−3/2)\arcsin(-\sqrt3/2)arcsin(−3​/2) unevaluated: inverse trigonometric functions at the standard exact values are not simplified (though cos⁡(arctan⁡34)=45\cos(\arctan\tfrac34) = \tfrac45cos(arctan43​)=54​ is).
PartlyPrecalculus and trigonometry 15
Ellipse
The curve is recognised as an ellipse, but no function gives its vertices, foci, axes or eccentricity.
PartlyPrecalculus and trigonometry 16
Hyperbola
Recognised as a hyperbola; foci and asymptotes are not computed.
PartlyPrecalculus and trigonometry 17
Parametric curves
symbolic confirms that the parametrisation satisfies the ellipse, but there is no elimination of a parameter (a resultant over sin and cos) to find the equation.
PartlyHigh-school statistics (AP Statistics) 15
Confidence interval for a mean
meanInterval takes raw data only, not a summary (n, mean, s) as textbook problems give it, so the interval is assembled from the ttt quantile by hand.
PartlyCalculus I 7
Implicit differentiation
There is no implicit differentiation; the code uses dy/dx=−Fx/Fydy/dx = -F_x/F_ydy/dx=−Fx​/Fy​, which the student has to know.
PartlyCalculus I 15
Newton's method
analytic's newton teaches the method in floating point (true errors, observed order); the exact iterates here are written as a loop over Core rationals.
PartlyCalculus I 18
Fundamental theorem
No rule for differentiating an integral with a variable limit; the code integrates first and then differentiates.
PartlyCalculus II 19
Remainder bound
taylorRemainder gives Lagrange's bound and the true maximum error (shown in the printed line); the value 1.105 is read from the polynomial.
PartlyCalculus III: several variables and vector calculus 1
Dot and cross products
No library has a cross product in three dimensions (geo's cross is the planar one, and is not exported). The dot product is a 1×3 by 3×1 matrix product, and the cross product is built from 2×2 determinants by hand.
PartlyCalculus III: several variables and vector calculus 2
Plane through three points
No plane object: the plane ax+by+cz+d=0ax + by + cz + d = 0ax+by+cz+d=0 through the points is the null space of the matrix of rows (x,y,z,1)(x, y, z, 1)(x,y,z,1).
PartlyCalculus III: several variables and vector calculus 9
Reversing the order
The order of integration is reversed by hand; the libraries do not change the order over a region.
PartlyOrdinary differential equations 20
Newton's law of cooling
Each step is available (the general solution, and kkk from the data), but the final value is assembled by hand.
PartlyProbability 7
A continuous density
stats has the named continuous families but no distribution from a user's density, so the moments are integrals done with symbolic.
PartlyProbability 17
A transformed variable
No change-of-variables for distributions: the CDF y\sqrt yy​ is worked out by hand, then differentiated.
PartlyProbability 18
Order statistics
The density of the maximum is derived by hand; there is no order-statistic tool.
PartlyMathematical statistics 6
Interval for a variance
varianceInterval takes raw data only, so with just nnn and s2s^2s2 the interval is built by hand from the chi-square quantiles.
PartlyMathematical statistics 14
Power of a z test
No power or sample-size function for the standard tests (only the simulation-based powerCurve); the power is assembled from normal quantiles and the CDF.
PartlyMathematical statistics 15
Sample size
Done by hand from the normal quantile; there is no sample-size helper. (ceil() returns a bigint.)
