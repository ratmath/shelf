Course Test · University: upper level

Financial mathematics: test

TestIndependent answersRatMath answers

Effective and continuous rates, annuities and perpetuities, amortised loans, net present value and internal rate of return, bond prices, yields and duration, forward rates, Black–Scholes and binomial option prices, option strategies, depreciation and a first life table.

Twenty exercises of the kind this course sets. Answers are on the independent answers page; the same exercises done with RatMath are on the RatMath answers page.

  1. 1.Effective rate
    What effective annual rate corresponds to 6% compounded monthly?
  2. 2.Compound interest
    What does \$1000 grow to in 10 years at 5% compounded annually?
  3. 3.Continuous compounding
    What does \$1000 grow to in 10 years at 5% compounded continuously?
  4. 4.Annuity-immediate
    Find the present value of \$100 paid at the end of each year for 10 years at 5%.
  5. 5.Annuity-due
    The same payments are made at the start of each year instead. Find the present value.
  6. 6.Growing perpetuity
    A perpetuity pays \$100 next year, growing by 2% a year after that. At 6%, what is it worth?
  7. 7.Loan payment
    Find the monthly payment on a \$250{,}000, 30-year loan at 6% a year compounded monthly.
  8. 8.Paying extra
    With \$200 a month extra on the same loan, how many payments does it take?
  9. 9.Net present value
    A project costs \$1000 now and returns \$300, \$400 and \$500 at the ends of the next three years. Find its NPV at 10%.
  10. 10.Internal rate of return
    Find the internal rate of return of the same project.
  11. 11.Bond price
    A 10-year bond with face 100 pays a 5% coupon semi-annually. Price it at a yield of 6% (compounded semi-annually).
  12. 12.Yield at par
    At what yield does the same bond sell at par?
  13. 13.Duration
    Find the Macaulay and modified durations of the bond at a 6% yield.
  14. 14.Forward rates
    The one-year spot rate is 4% and the two-year spot rate is 5%. Find the one-year forward rate a year from now.
  15. 15.Black–Scholes
    Price a one-year European call with S=K=100S = K = 100S=K=100, r=5%r = 5\%r=5%, σ=20%\sigma = 20\%σ=20%.
  16. 16.Put–call parity
    Use put–call parity to price the matching put.
  17. 17.American put on a tree
    A stock at 50 moves up by 1.2 or down by 0.8 each step; one step grows money by 1.05. Price a two-step American put struck at 52.
  18. 18.Option strategies
    A straddle buys a call (premium 5) and a put (premium 4), both struck at 100. Where does it break even?
  19. 19.Depreciation
    A machine costs \$10{,}000, lasts 5 years and is sold for \$1000. Find the yearly straight-line depreciation and the first year's double-declining-balance depreciation.
  20. 20.Life expectancy
    Under de Moivre's law with limiting age 100, what is the complete life expectancy at age 40?
