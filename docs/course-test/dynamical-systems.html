Dynamical systems and chaos: test

TestIndependent answersRatMath answers

Fixed points and multipliers of the logistic map, its 2-cycles, the first period doublings and the superstable 2-cycle, the Lyapunov exponent at (r = 4), rational orbits of the tent and doubling maps, Sharkovskii's order, fixed points of the Hénon map, the Lorenz equilibria and their types, the damped pendulum, Arnold's cat map (periodic points and entropy), dimensions of the Cantor set, the Sierpinski triangle and a Moran set, membership in the Mandelbrot set, and oscillators and spaceships in the Game of Life.

Twenty exercises of the kind this course sets. Answers are on the independent answers page; the same exercises done with RatMath are on the RatMath answers page.

  1. 1.Fixed points and stability
    Find the fixed points of the logistic map f(x)=3x(1−x)f(x) = 3x(1 - x)f(x)=3x(1−x) and classify them.
  2. 2.Multipliers
    For f(x)=52x(1−x)f(x) = \tfrac52x(1 - x)f(x)=25​x(1−x), find the non-zero fixed point and its multiplier.
  3. 3.Period-2 orbits
    Find the 2-cycle of f(x)=4x(1−x)f(x) = 4x(1 - x)f(x)=4x(1−x) and its stability.
  4. 4.Period doubling
    At which rrr do the first two period doublings of the logistic map occur?
  5. 5.Superstable cycles
    Find the rrr at which the logistic map has a superstable 2-cycle (the critical point 12\tfrac1221​ has period 2).
  6. 6.Lyapunov exponent
    What is the Lyapunov exponent of the logistic map at r=4r = 4r=4?
  7. 7.Tent map orbits
    Find the orbit of 15\tfrac1551​ under the tent map T(x)=2min⁡(x,1−x)T(x) = 2\min(x, 1 - x)T(x)=2min(x,1−x).
  8. 8.The doubling map
    Find the orbit of 17\tfrac1771​ under x↦2x mod 1x \mapsto 2x \bmod 1x↦2xmod1.
  9. 9.Sharkovskii's theorem
    For a continuous map of the line, does a 3-cycle force a 5-cycle? Does a 5-cycle force a 3-cycle?
  10. 10.The Hénon map
    Find the fixed points of the Hénon map (x,y)↦(1−1.4x2+y, 0.3x)(x, y) \mapsto (1 - 1.4x^2 + y,\ 0.3x)(x,y)↦(1−1.4x2+y, 0.3x).
  11. 11.Lorenz equilibria
    Find the equilibria of the Lorenz system with σ=10\sigma = 10σ=10, ρ=28\rho = 28ρ=28, β=83\beta = \tfrac83β=38​, and classify them.
  12. 12.The damped pendulum
    For θ′′+12θ′+sin⁡θ=0\theta'' + \tfrac12\theta' + \sin\theta = 0θ′′+21​θ′+sinθ=0, classify the equilibria with −4≤θ≤4-4 \le \theta \le 4−4≤θ≤4.
  13. 13.Arnold's cat map
    For the cat map (2111)\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}(21​11​) on the torus, how many fixed points and how many points of period dividing 2 are there? What is its entropy?
  14. 14.Cantor set
    Find the box-counting dimension of the middle-thirds Cantor set.
  15. 15.Sierpinski triangle
    Find the similarity dimension of the Sierpinski triangle.
  16. 16.Moran's equation
    A self-similar set has two pieces, scaled by 12\tfrac1221​ and 14\tfrac1441​. Find its dimension.
  17. 17.The Mandelbrot set
    Are c=−1c = -1c=−1 and c=1c = 1c=1 in the Mandelbrot set?
  18. 18.Game of Life
    Classify the blinker and the glider in Conway's Game of Life.
  19. 19.Sensitive dependence
    For the tent map T(x)=2min⁡(x,1−x)T(x) = 2\min(x, 1 - x)T(x)=2min(x,1−x), the points 13\tfrac1331​ and 13+10−6\tfrac13 + 10^{-6}31​+10−6 start close. After how many steps are they more than 110\tfrac1{10}101​ apart?
  20. 20.Itineraries
    Under f(x)=4x(1−x)f(x) = 4x(1 - x)f(x)=4x(1−x), write the first 6 symbols of the itinerary of x0=15x_0 = \tfrac15x0​=51​ (L if x<12x < \tfrac12x<21​, R if x>12x > \tfrac12x>21​).
