TestIndependent answersRatMath answers

Propositional logic and equivalences, inclusion–exclusion, permutations and combinations with repetition, derangements, linear recurrences, sums proved by induction, modular arithmetic, the Euclidean algorithm and Bézout, the Chinese remainder theorem, Euler circuits, shortest paths, spanning trees, Cayley's formula, equivalence relations, number bases and the pigeonhole principle.

Twenty exercises of the kind this course sets. Answers are on the independent answers page; the same exercises done with RatMath are on the RatMath answers page.
