Arrangements of MISSISSIPPI, multisets, inclusion–exclusion on divisibility, derangements, surjections, Stirling numbers of both kinds, Bell, Eulerian, Catalan and partition numbers, compositions, Dyck words, Vandermonde's identity, linear recurrences solved in closed form, making change with a generating function, labelled trees, Burnside and Pólya counting of coloured squares, and the Fano plane as a Steiner triple system.
