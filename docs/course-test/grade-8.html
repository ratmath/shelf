Grade 8: linear equations, functions and the Pythagorean theorem: test

TestIndependent answersRatMath answers

Integer exponents and scientific notation, square roots and irrational numbers, linear equations with one, none or infinitely many solutions, slope and lines, systems of two equations, the Pythagorean theorem and distance, volumes of cylinders, cones and spheres, transformations, angle facts and lines of best fit.

Twenty exercises of the kind this course sets. Answers are on the independent answers page; the same exercises done with RatMath are on the RatMath answers page.

    (3.2×105)(4×10−2)(3.2 \times 10^5)(4 \times 10^{-2})(3.2×105)(4×10−2). Write the answer in scientific notation.
