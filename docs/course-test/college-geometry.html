College geometry: test

TestIndependent answersRatMath answers

Circumcentre, incentre, orthocentre and centroid of a right triangle, the Euler line and the 2 : 1 ratio, the nine-point circle, inversion in a circle, the cross ratio and harmonic conjugates, the conic through five points, composing reflections, Pick's theorem, interior angles of regular polygons, a spherical octant and its excess, great-circle distance, hyperbolic distance and the defect of hyperbolic and ideal triangles, the Fano plane and the projective plane of order 3, Euler's formula for the icosahedron, and the taxicab metric.

Twenty exercises of the kind this course sets. Answers are on the independent answers page; the same exercises done with RatMath are on the RatMath answers page.

  1. 1.Triangle centres
    For the triangle A(0,0)A(0,0)A(0,0), B(4,0)B(4,0)B(4,0), C(0,3)C(0,3)C(0,3), find the circumcentre and circumradius, the incentre and inradius, the orthocentre and the centroid.
  2. 2.The Euler line
    For the same triangle, show the circumcentre OOO, centroid GGG and orthocentre HHH are collinear with HG=2 GOHG = 2\,GOHG=2GO, and find the line.
  3. 3.Nine-point circle
    Find the centre and radius of the nine-point circle of the same triangle.
  4. 4.Inversion
    Find the images of (2,0)(2, 0)(2,0) and (1,1)(1, 1)(1,1) under inversion in the unit circle.
  5. 5.Cross ratio
    Find the cross ratio (A,B;C,D)=AC⋅BDBC⋅AD(A, B; C, D) = \dfrac{AC\cdot BD}{BC\cdot AD}(A,B;C,D)=BC⋅ADAC⋅BD​ of the points 0, 1, 2, 3 on a line.
  6. 6.Harmonic conjugates
    On a line, A=0A = 0A=0, B=3B = 3B=3, C=1C = 1C=1. Find the harmonic conjugate DDD of CCC with respect to A,BA, BA,B.
  7. 7.Conic through five points
    Find the conic through (±1,0)(\pm1, 0)(±1,0), (0,±2)(0, \pm2)(0,±2) and (35,85)(\tfrac35, \tfrac85)(53​,58​), and classify it.
  8. 8.Composing reflections
    Reflect in the line y=xy = xy=x, then in the xxx-axis. What single isometry is this?
  9. 9.Pick's theorem
    The rectangle with vertices (0,0)(0,0)(0,0), (4,0)(4,0)(4,0), (4,3)(4,3)(4,3), (0,3)(0,3)(0,3) has how many interior lattice points? Check Pick's theorem.
  10. 10.Regular polygons
    Find the interior angle of a regular 12-gon, as a fraction of a full turn and in degrees.
  11. 11.Spherical triangles
    On the unit sphere, take the north pole and the equator points at longitudes 0° and 90°. Find the angle sum, excess and area of the triangle.
  12. 12.Great-circle distance
    On the unit sphere, how far apart are the points at latitude 0°, longitudes 0° and 90°?
  13. 13.Hyperbolic distance
    In the Poincaré disc, find the distance from the centre to the point (12,0)(\tfrac12, 0)(21​,0).
  14. 14.Hyperbolic triangles
    In the Poincaré disc, find the angle sum and area of the triangle with vertices 000, (12,0)(\tfrac12, 0)(21​,0), (0,12)(0, \tfrac12)(0,21​).
  15. 15.Ideal triangles
    What is the area of a hyperbolic triangle whose three vertices are on the boundary circle?
  16. 16.Finite projective planes
    The Fano plane is the projective plane of order 2. How many lines does it have? How many points has the projective plane of order 3?
  17. 17.Euler's formula for polyhedra
    Check Euler's formula for the icosahedron.
  18. 18.Taxicab geometry
    Find the taxicab and Euclidean distances between (1,2)(1, 2)(1,2) and (4,6)(4, 6)(4,6).
  19. 19.Euclidean theorem proving
    Prove that the nine-point circle passes through the midpoints of the sides, the feet of the altitudes, and the midpoints from the orthocentre to the vertices, for every triangle.
  20. 20.Sides and angles
    For the triangle A(0,0)A(0,0)A(0,0), B(4,0)B(4,0)B(4,0), C(0,3)C(0,3)C(0,3), find the side lengths and the angle at BBB in degrees.
